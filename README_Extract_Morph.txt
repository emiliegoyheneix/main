Drag and drop the Extract_Morph.ms file into the viewport.

This script extract your morher in animation pose.

Steps:
1. Select your model skin.
2. Duplicate it and collapse all under the skin modifier.
3. Delete all on top the skin modifier.
4. Do your scult.
5. Pick your skin model with the pickbutton.
6. Pick your deform model with the pickbutton.
7. Choose name if you want it(uncheched the default checkbox for it).
8. Click on "Create" button.
